# Consistent title typography

## Changes
- Use the modern sans-serif interface font for all page, section, panel, and card titles.
- Standardize the hierarchy:
  - Main page title: 36px desktop, 30px mobile, semibold
  - Major section title: 24px, semibold
  - Panel title: 20px, semibold
  - Card title: 18px, semibold
- Keep the large public homepage headline as the only editorial display treatment; it is intentionally promotional rather than an interface title.
- Apply the shared title styles across public pages, student pages, teacher pages, admin, authentication, course cards, activities, and error states.
- Remove inconsistent letter-spacing rules and ensure long titles wrap cleanly on mobile.

## Verification
- Check the main public and signed-in screens at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm all title levels remain readable, aligned, and free of overflow.
