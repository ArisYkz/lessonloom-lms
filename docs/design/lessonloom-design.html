Screens Public Student Teacher

Product plan · v1 draft · 2026-10-13

LessonLoom: who it is for, what each page does, how people move through it

This is a plan, not a finished design. Every screen below maps to a route that exists in the codebase today, or to a route marked new. Section 6 lists the decisions I need from you.

1. Who it is for

Primary: individual teachers. The data model already treats one teacher account as one organization (organizations, one owner, staff can be added later). A tutor who creates courses, invites students by a link, and pays for a plan is the core customer. Teachers are also the supply side: without them the catalog is empty.

Volume: students. Many people browse, enroll, and join classes, usually from a phone. They need discovery, a clear join path, and a calendar they will actually open.

Internal: admins. Existing /admin. Not part of the design.

Design decision: design the student experience for first impressions and the teacher experience for daily work. Both are equally important, but the teacher console is where the product earns money, so it must feel calm and fast rather than decorative.

3. Workflows

Teacher: from zero to a running class

1Sign up, set name, photo, headline on the profile.
2Create a course (draft). Add sections and lessons.
3Publish the course. Students can now find it.
4Create a class on that course. Copy the join link.
5Add sessions to the class calendar (new feature).
6Students join. Review homework in the inbox.

Student: from link to lesson

AGets a link /join/CODE from the teacher, or searches the catalog.
BSees the teacher, class, course, and classmates. Signs in or signs up.
CJoins the class. The course appears on My learning.
DOpens the calendar to see when sessions happen and who is coming.
ELearns, submits homework, earns progress.
FLeaves the class if needed. Access ends with it.

Student: browsing

Landing or catalog → search or filter → open a course or teacher → read the curriculum → enroll (free) or pay → the course appears in My learning. Teachers appear as cards; each course card shows the teacher's photo so people can recognize a teacher they already like.

5. What is missing from the data model

Sessions (the calendar) do not exist yet. A class today is a group with a join code, with no time attached. Your calendar idea needs a new table, for example class_sessions (classroom_id, title, starts_at, duration_min, time zone, meeting_url optional), with the same RLS as classroom members. This is a migration and tests, like the classroom work. It is the single biggest gap between the current app and the design.

Terminology: in the app, "class" means a group with a join code (already built). "Session" means one scheduled meeting in a class (new). The calendar shows sessions; the classes page shows classes.

6. Decisions (confirmed)

  1. Primary user: individual teachers. Students are designed for as well.
  2. Mood: warm paper background, deep teal accent, serif display headings, Inter for UI.
  3. Searchable classes: private by default. A teacher can tick "list publicly" to show a class in search and on their profile. Joining a listed class still asks the student to confirm.
  4. Classmate photos: visible to classmates. A profile setting lets a student hide their photo.
  5. "Who is coming": members of the class for v1. RSVPs come later as a separate table.
  6. Calendar scope: one calendar per student and one per teacher, covering all their classes. Time zone is stored per session and shown in the viewer's time zone.
  7. Live meetings: a meeting link only. We do not host video.

7. Build order

  1. Design system: tokens, buttons, cards, avatars, search bar, empty and loading states. Light and dark. Check Russian and Kazakh text length in every component.
  2. Public: landing, catalog with search and filters, course page, teacher profile.
  3. Student: merge home into My learning, My classes, join-by-link page.
  4. Schedule (backend first): class_sessions migration, RLS, tests. Then calendar for students and teachers.
  5. Teacher console: teacher home, class page, course Classes tab, plan & limits.
  6. Quality pass: mobile at 390px, keyboard focus, aria-labels, no hardcoded strings, a browser golden-path run with screenshots.

Every new string goes through src/i18n/dictionary.ts in English, Russian, and Kazakh. Russian and Kazakh need a native review.
